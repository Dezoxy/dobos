# Contributing

Dobos is maintained by one person for their own use.

- **Issues are welcome**: bug reports with logs and the steps that led
  there, and ideas.
- **Pull requests are opened by the maintainer only.** The repository
  restricts who can open one. If you have a fix, describe it in an issue.
- **Vulnerabilities** go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public
  issue.

What the project is for, and what it will not grow into, is in the
[README](README.md) and [docs/ideas.md](docs/ideas.md).
